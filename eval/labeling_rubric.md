# Risk labeling rules (customer's point of view)

Label each clause Low, Medium or High. This is decision support, not legal advice.
The customer is the party buying or receiving the service. The `customer` column names it.
Use only the text shown. Do not guess facts that are not written there.

## Governing Law
- Low: the customer's own state or country, or a neutral law that neither party is based in
- Medium: the other party's state or country (same country as the customer), or the text does not show where the customer is
- High: a law or exclusive court or arbitration in a different country from the customer, and not neutral

## Renewal Term
- Low: no automatic renewal, or it renews for 1 month or less
- Medium: auto-renews for more than 1 month, up to 1 year
- High: auto-renews for more than 1 year

## Notice Period To Terminate Renewal
- Low: 30 days or less
- Medium: 31 to 60 days
- High: more than 60 days, or no way to stop the renewal

## Termination For Convenience
- Low: both sides can end it without a reason on 60 days notice or less and no fee, or only the customer can
- Medium: both can, but the customer pays a fee or must give more than 60 days notice
- High: only the provider can end it, or the customer cannot end it without a reason
- A right to end it only for breach is not this clause. Label NA.

## Non-Compete
- Low: binds only the provider, or lasts only during the contract and covers only the products in it
- Medium: binds the customer, ends within 12 months after the contract, and is limited to named products or a named area
- High: binds the customer and lasts more than 12 months after the contract, or covers a whole industry or the whole world with no product or area limit
- Confidentiality or non-solicit duties are not a non-compete. Label NA.

## Cap On Liability
- Low: a mutual cap of at least 12 months of fees, with carve-outs for at least gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Medium: the provider's cap is 3 to under 12 months of fees, or the cap is 12 months or more but has no carve-outs
- High: the provider's cap is under 3 months of fees, or the provider has no liability, or only the customer's liability is uncapped

## Uncapped Liability
- Low: unlimited only for the provider, or unlimited for both sides only for fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, or death and personal injury
- Medium: unlimited for both sides for broader things, such as confidentiality, data or IP
- High: unlimited only for the customer, or for any breach by the customer

## Indemnification
- Low: the provider covers the customer (for example IP infringement), or both cover each other only for third-party claims
- Medium: both cover each other, but it goes beyond third-party claims or the process is unclear
- High: only the customer covers the provider, or it covers the provider's own negligence

## Rules
- If two levels fit, pick the higher risk and set unsure to 1.
- If the text is not this clause, write NA.
- Write one short reason for every label.
- Do not look at the model's answer while labeling.
- Do not change this file after you start labeling. If you change it, relabel everything.
