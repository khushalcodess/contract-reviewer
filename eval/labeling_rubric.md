# Risk labeling rules (customer's point of view)

Label each clause Low, Medium or High. Not legal advice.

## Governing Law
- Low: customer's home state or a neutral, well-known law
- Medium: provider's state, or unclear
- High: far-away or unusual law, or exclusive far-away court

## Renewal Term
- Low: no auto-renewal, or renews for 1 month or less
- Medium: auto-renews for up to 1 year
- High: auto-renews for more than 1 year

## Notice Period To Terminate Renewal
- Low: 30 days or less
- Medium: 31 to 60 days
- High: more than 60 days, or no way to stop renewal

## Termination For Convenience
- Low: both sides can end it with short notice
- Medium: both can end it but customer pays a fee or gives long notice
- High: only provider can end it, or customer cannot end it at all

## Non-Compete
- Low: none
- Medium: limited by time and place
- High: broad, long, or hard to avoid

## Cap On Liability
- Low: fair cap that covers the customer's real loss
- Medium: low cap, or covers only fees paid
- High: provider has a tiny cap or no liability, customer has none

## Uncapped Liability
- Low: unlimited only for the provider, or for normal things (fraud)
- Medium: unlimited for both sides
- High: unlimited only for the customer

## Indemnification
- Low: provider protects customer, or both protect each other fairly
- Medium: both protect each other but unclear
- High: only customer must protect provider

## Rules
- If you are unsure between two levels, pick the higher risk.
- Write one short reason for every label.
- Use the same rules every time.
